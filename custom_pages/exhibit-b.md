**Exhibit B**

**Revenue Share**

1. Revenue Sharing Generally.

   1. Channel Partner agrees to integrate Prahsys’s payment processing services into its platform to process its customer transactions. Channel Partner will receive a portion of the Net Revenue generated through its platform(s) equal to the Net Revenue Percentage, as set forth in this Exhibit B.

   2. For purposes of the revenue sharing arrangement between the Parties, Net Revenue shall be the total revenue generated through processing customer transactions by Prahsys and any existing or future parent, subsidiary, or affiliate of Prahsys through Channel Partner’s platform, less the direct expenses associated with processing such transactions, which shall include without limitation payment network fees, interchange rates, conversion rates, and any expenses directly related to payment processing as determined by Prahsys in its sole discretion.
2. Payments of Quarterly Revenue Share; Late Payments.

   1. Payments of Quarterly Revenue Share. Commencing with the first full calendar quarter immediately following the Effective Date, Channel Partner shall be entitled to receive a payment from Prahsys for each calendar quarter in an amount equal to the Quarterly Revenue Share. All payments of the Quarterly Revenue Share shall be paid by Prahsys to Channel Partner via an electronic ACH transfer, to a bank account designated by Channel Partner, within thirty (30) calendar days after the last day of any calendar quarter during which any Quarterly Gross Revenues attributable to the Quarterly Revenue Share are received by Prahsys.

   2. Payment Information for Other Payments. Channel Partner agrees to identify a bank account to receive all payments owed to it under this Agreement in addition to the Quarterly Revenue Share payment. This bank account may be the same bank account that Channel Partner utilizes to receive the Quarterly Revenue Share payment. Channel Partner agrees to provide Prahsys with the bank account information necessary for Prahsys to make such payments, including the Quarterly Revenue Share payment, to Channel Partner and shall update Prahsys with any changes to such information.

   3. Interest Rate. No payment of the Quarterly Revenue Share shall be allowed to have an interest payment that exceeds any statutory maximum interest rate imposed by federal or state law. To the extent that a payment of the Quarterly Revenue Share has an estimated interest rate that exceeds a statutory maximum interest rate imposed by federal or state law, Prahsys will reduce the payment of the Quarterly Revenue Share to the maximum payment allowable under federal or state laws. The immediate future payments of the Quarterly Revenue Share will include any unpaid amounts until there is no balance of any unpaid amounts.

   4. Late Payments. To the extent that any payment of the Quarterly Revenue Share is not paid within fifteen (15) business days of such payment becoming due to Channel Partner, and the delay is not excused, Prahsys shall be assessed a late payment charge at an annual rate equal to three percent (3.0%) based on the number of days elapsed out of a 365-day calendar year. A delay shall be excused under this paragraph to the extent it is due to events outside of Prahsys’s control, including without limitation an act of God or the actions or inactions of Prahsys’s third-party service provider, or Channel Partner, provided that Prahsys will take all reasonable efforts to make the payment as soon as practicable. This late payment charge shall be cumulative and assessed once per quarter against the unpaid amounts due to Channel Partner from Prahsys from the due date until the date of payment thereof and shall accrue and be added to any balance of unpaid amounts subject to late payment.
