### **EXHIBIT F**

This Exhibit F is attached to and made part of that certain Master Service Agreement (the "Agreement") by and between Prahsys Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Prahsys"), and the channel partner identified in the Agreement ("Channel Partner").

**1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE**

This Exhibit establishes user interface and user experience requirements that Channel Partner shall implement when integrating Prahsys payment processing services into Channel Partner's application or platform. These requirements are designed to ensure merchants have appropriate visibility into payment processing status, access to necessary payment management tools, and a compliant onboarding experience.

**2. DEFINITIONS**

When used in this Exhibit, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

**"Merchant"** means any customer of Channel Partner who utilizes payment processing services provided by Prahsys through Channel Partner's platform.

**"Channel Partner Application"** means the software application, platform, or system operated by Channel Partner through which Merchants access services, including payment processing services provided by Prahsys.

**"Custom Onboarding"** means a merchant onboarding experience designed and implemented by Channel Partner within the Channel Partner Application, as opposed to redirecting Merchants to Prahsys-hosted onboarding interfaces.

**3. MANDATORY UI/UX REQUIREMENTS**

**3.1 Merchant Application Status Visibility**

Channel Partner shall implement and maintain a dedicated user interface element within the Channel Partner Application that provides Merchants with real-time visibility into the status of their payment processing applications. This requirement includes the following specifications:

(a) **Accessibility**: The status interface shall be readily accessible to Merchants without requiring navigation outside the Channel Partner Application's primary environment.

(b) Status **Updates**: Status information shall reflect the current onboarding stage as provided by Prahsys's systems, including but not limited to: Application Submitted, Under Review, Additional Information Required, Approved, and Active. Channel Partner shall update such status information within a commercially reasonable timeframe after receiving updates from Prahsys.

(c) **Continuous Availability**: Status visibility shall be maintained throughout the entire merchant lifecycle, from initial application through active processing status.

**3.2 Prahsys Dashboard Access**

Channel Partner shall implement a clearly identifiable navigation element within the Channel Partner Application that provides Merchants with direct access to [dashboard.prahsys.com](http://www.dashboard.prahsys.com/). This requirement includes the following specifications:

(a) **Navigation Element**: Channel Partner shall implement a clearly labeled button, link, or other navigation element that directs Merchants to dashboard.prahsys.com.

(b) **Persistent Accessibility**: The navigation element shall be persistently accessible from the payments management section of the Channel Partner Application.

(c) **Purpose**: This navigation element enables Merchants to access comprehensive payment management tools, reporting capabilities, and account administration features provided by Prahsys.

**3.3 Custom Merchant Onboarding – Entity Type Requirements**

In the event Channel Partner elects to implement Custom Onboarding rather than redirecting Merchants to Prahsys-hosted onboarding interfaces, Channel Partner shall implement data collection and validation capabilities for all of the following entity types:

(a) **Limited Liability Company (LLC)**: A business structure that combines the liability protection of a corporation with the tax flexibility of a partnership, where owners are called "members."

(b) **Corporation**: A separate legal entity owned by shareholders that provides maximum liability protection but is subject to corporate income tax in addition to personal taxes on dividends.

(c) **Government**: A federal, state, or local government agency or other governmental entity operating under public authority.

(d) **Sole Proprietor**: An unincorporated business owned and operated by one individual where there is no legal distinction between the owner and the business entity.

(e) **Public Company**: A corporation whose ownership shares are traded on public stock exchanges and must comply with Securities and Exchange Commission reporting requirements.

(f) **Non-Profit Organization**: An organization incorporated to serve a charitable, educational, religious, or other public purpose where profits are reinvested rather than distributed to owners.

(g) **Joint Stock Company**: A business entity where ownership is divided into transferable shares, similar to a corporation but typically with unlimited liability for shareholders.

(h) **Partnership**: A business owned by two or more individuals who share profits, losses, and management responsibilities without incorporating as a separate legal entity.

Channel Partner's Custom Onboarding interface shall collect all entity-type-specific information required by Prahsys payment processing underwriting standards for each applicable entity type.

**3.4 Custom Merchant Onboarding – Merchant Agreement Execution**

In the event Channel Partner elects to implement Custom Onboarding, Channel Partner shall integrate electronic signature functionality within the Channel Partner Application to facilitate execution of Prahsys Merchant Processing Agreements. This requirement includes the following specifications:

(a) **Integration Requirement**: Channel Partner shall integrate DocuSign or other electronic signature platform approved in writing by Prahsys to enable execution of required merchant agreements.

(b) Integrated **Experience**: The integration shall enable Merchants to review, execute, and submit required merchant agreements through the Channel Partner Application, which may include embedded interfaces or secure redirects that maintain session continuity.

(c) **Purpose**: This integration is intended to expedite merchant onboarding timelines and improve conversion rates by reducing friction in the agreement execution process.

**4. COMPLIANCE AND QUALITY STANDARDS**

Channel Partner shall ensure that all implementations under this Exhibit comply with the following standards:

**4.1 Accessibility Requirements**

All user interface implementations required by this Exhibit shall meet or exceed Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards or such other industry-standard accessibility requirements as may be specified by Prahsys from time to time.

**4.2 Usability Standards**

All interface elements required by this Exhibit shall be clearly labeled, intuitive for end-users, and consistent with generally accepted user experience best practices for commercial software applications.

**4.3 Ongoing Maintenance**

Channel Partner shall maintain all UI/UX implementations required by this Exhibit in good working order throughout the term of the Agreement. Channel Partner shall remediate any deficiencies in required implementations within five (5) business days after receiving written notice from Prahsys specifying the deficiency. If remediation cannot reasonably be completed within such timeframe due to technical complexity, Channel Partner shall provide Prahsys with a remediation plan and timeline within such five (5) business day period.

**4.4 Updates and Modifications**

Prahsys may update the requirements set forth in this Exhibit from time to time by providing written notice to Channel Partner. Channel Partner shall implement required updates within thirty (30) days of receiving such notice, or such other timeframe as mutually agreed by the parties in writing.

**5. VERIFICATION AND AUDIT RIGHTS**

Prahsys reserves the right to verify Channel Partner's compliance with the requirements of this Exhibit through testing, inspection, or audit of the Channel Partner Application, provided that: (i) Prahsys provides at least ten (10) business days' prior written notice of any such audit or inspection; (ii) such audits or inspections are conducted no more than once per calendar year unless Prahsys has reasonable grounds to believe a material breach has occurred; (iii) such activities are conducted during Channel Partner's normal business hours and in a manner that minimizes disruption to Channel Partner's operations; and (iv) Prahsys bears its own costs for such audits unless material non-compliance is identified. Channel Partner shall reasonably cooperate with any such verification activities and shall remediate any material deficiencies within a reasonable timeframe after receiving written notice specifying the deficiency with reasonable particularity.
