### **Shared Scan Access Terms**

**Last Updated: July 20, 2026**

Your dental provider has used PrognosiX, a platform operated by Prahsys, Inc. (“Prahsys,” “we,” or “us”), to share dental imaging with you. By accessing imaging shared with you through PrognosiX, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not access the imaging, and contact your dental provider. These terms apply only to this optional shared-viewing feature. You can always request your imaging from your dental provider directly, regardless of these terms.

***

#### **For Your Information Only; Not a Diagnosis**

The imaging shared with you, including any AI-generated visualization such as a highlighted pulp-tissue overlay, is provided for your general information and to help you discuss your care with your dental provider. It is not medical advice and is not a diagnosis. Any AI-generated visualization is produced by investigational software that is still in development, has not been cleared or approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, and has not been shown to be safe or effective for any purpose. Please do not rely on it for any health decision. Always talk to your dental provider, who is responsible for interpreting your imaging and for your care.

***

#### **Access Is Personal to You**

Any link or access we provide is intended only for you or your authorized representative. Please keep it private, and do not forward, post, or publicly share it. Access may expire, and it may be turned off by your provider or by us at any time. If you are accessing this imaging on behalf of a patient as their parent, legal guardian, or personal representative, you confirm that you have the legal authority to do so.

***

#### **Please Use It Responsibly**

When accessing shared imaging, you agree not to:

* attempt to access imaging, accounts, or data that were not shared with you;
* copy, scrape, reverse engineer, or interfere with the platform or its security;
* use the platform to harass others or for any unlawful purpose; or
* misrepresent your identity.

We may suspend or disable access if we reasonably believe it is being misused or presents a security or privacy risk.

***

#### **Your Imaging and Your Rights**

Your imaging is part of the medical record that your dental provider maintains as the entity responsible for your care under HIPAA. To obtain a copy of your records, request a correction, ask how your information is used, or ask that a shared link be disabled, please contact your dental provider directly. Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under HIPAA or other applicable law. Prahsys processes your imaging only to make it available to you, on your provider’s instruction; we do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train our AI models. Our handling of your information is described in our [Privacy Policy](https://docs.prahsys.com/page/privacy-policy).

***

#### **Questions or Concerns**

If you received a link that was not meant for you, please do not view, use, copy, or share the imaging, and email us at [support@prahsys.com](mailto:support@prahsys.com) (or notify the dental provider named in the message) so we can disable access. If you believe someone else has accessed your imaging, please contact your dental provider or email us at [support@prahsys.com](mailto:support@prahsys.com). For questions about your care or your imaging, please contact your dental provider.
