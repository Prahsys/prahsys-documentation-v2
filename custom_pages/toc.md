Channel Partner or Merchant remains solely responsible for any data security breaches or non-compliance resulting from improper data sanitization of surrendered terminals.

#### 2.6 Post-Termination

Upon termination of this Agreement for any reason, Channel Partner or Merchant must either: (i) return all terminals provided under the Free Terminal Program to Prahsys in good working condition, normal wear and tear excepted, or (ii) pay Prahsys the full retail cost of any unreturned terminals.
