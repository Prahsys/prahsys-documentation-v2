# **Definitions**

Unless otherwise defined in the Agreement, the terms set forth below shall have the following meanings:

“Administrative Agent” means a third party that, upon written notice from Prahsys to Channel Partner, shall provide administrative and data services on behalf of Prahsys and may become a party to this Agreement or a third-party beneficiary to this Agreement.

“Change in Control” means (a) Prahsys’s consummation of a merger, consolidation, reorganization, or similar business transaction, unless immediately after such transaction more than 50% of the outstanding voting power of the surviving or resulting entity is held by persons or entities who or that were members of Prahsys immediately before the transaction; or (b) Prahsys’s consummation of a sale of all or substantially all of its assets.

“Confidential Information” means, whether or not such information is designated or marked by Prahsys as confidential, proprietary or secret, (a) any and all financial, technical and other information regarding Prahsys and its business, products, assets or properties; and (b) any and all proprietary information, materials, know-how and trade secrets of Prahsys with regard to the ideas, technology, products, business or business methods (whether or not in written, electronic, machine readable or other tangible form) of Prahsys, any parent, subsidiary or affiliate of Prahsys, or any of their respective officers, directors, members, managers, employees or agents.

“Initial Term” shall have the meaning ascribed to it in Section 3 of the Agreement.

“Net Revenue” shall have the meaning ascribed to it in Section 1.b of Exhibit B.

“PGS” means Prahsys Gateway System.

“Practice Management Software” means any software used by a medical practitioner to run their practice including but not limited to storing patient data, managing appointments, storing HIPAA data and other data subject to sharing restrictions under other regulations, as well as integrated payment processing for transactions.

“Practitioner” means the medical practitioner(s) who are the user(s) of a Practice Management Software of Channel Partner.

“Prahsys Gateway System” means the technology provided to channel partners to provide integrated payment solutions to their practitioners.

“Predefined Use Cases” means the collecting, tokenizing, and storing of patient cards and authorizing information on behalf of practitioners on Channel Partner’s platform.

“Quarterly Gross Revenue” means all gross revenues collected by Prahsys and any existing or future parent, subsidiary, or affiliate of Prahsys during the Quarterly Revenue Sharing Period through Channel Partner’s Platform.

“Quarterly Revenue Share” means an amount determined for each Quarterly Revenue Sharing Period equal to Net Revenue for such Quarterly Revenue Sharing Period multiplied by the Revenue Percentage.

“Quarterly Revenue Sharing Period” means (i) the full or partial calendar quarter beginning on the Effective Date and ending on the last day of the calendar quarter, and (ii) each full calendar quarter thereafter until the termination of this Agreement, it being understood that that final Quarterly Revenue Sharing Period may be a partial calendar quarter.

“Revenue Percentage” means 30 basis points, or 0.30%.

“Renewal Term” shall have the meaning ascribed to it in Section 2 of the Agreement.

“Senior Creditor(s)” means the existing senior creditors of Prahsys, any commercial bank or other lending institution seeking to issue a secured loan to Prahsys in the future and excludes any members or managers of Prahsys.

“Subscription Agreement” means the Subscription Agreement delivered by Channel Partner to Prahsys in connection with this Agreement.

“Term” means the Initial Term and all Renewal Terms, as applicable.
