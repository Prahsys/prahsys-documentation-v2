The Parties agree that in the event that: (i) the MSA is terminated for any reason prior to the expiration of the Initial Term or the current Renewal Term; (ii) Company ceases to provide any of the services specified under the MSA; or (iii) Company ceases any material business operation or ceases to operate as a going concern, Channel Partner and Infinicept enter into a Product and Service Agreement containing substantially the same terms as this Agreement and the MSA, including with respect to the Revenue Sharing for the Services, and other terms as reasonably required to govern the provision of the services directly to Channel Partner, each as mutually agreed by the Parties through good faith discussions (the “Direct Agreement”).
